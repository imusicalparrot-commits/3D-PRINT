Privacy & Cookies Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-04

The short version

This site is a static catalog. It has no accounts, no registration, no comments and no newsletter. We do not collect, store or sell personal data, and we do not run our own advertising or analytics scripts.

Cookies

Filament Finds itself sets no cookies. However, when you click through to a merchant (for example, Creative Fabrica or AliExpress), their sites may set their own cookies for sessions, carts and affiliate attribution. Those cookies are governed by each merchant's own privacy policy, not by this one.

Affiliate disclosure

Outbound product links on this site are affiliate links. If you make a purchase after clicking one, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate tracking is handled by the merchant's platform and typically relies on a short-lived cookie or URL parameter set on their domain after your click.

Server logs

Like virtually all websites, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) for security and troubleshooting. We do not use these logs to profile visitors and we do not enrich them with other data.

Third-party content

Product images are served from the merchant's CDN and remain the property of their respective authors. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of the request; if you prefer to avoid that, the site remains fully readable with system fonts when offline.

Your choices

  • Browse without clicking outbound links and nothing leaves the site.
  • Use your browser's tracking protection — the site works fine with strict settings.
  • Questions about this policy? The contact channel of the site owner is the place to ask.
