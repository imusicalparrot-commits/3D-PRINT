Easter Crafts to Print With Kids

2026-09-044 min read

Easter is the easiest holiday to turn into a printing craft session, because everything about it is small, chunky and forgiving. The printer does the hard part; the kids do the fun part.

The session plan

  • Print (adults, evening before): a batch of farm animals, a bunny or two, an egg holder. All flat-bottomed, all fast — the Easter & Spring collection is built for this.
  • Paint (kids, Saturday morning): acrylics, one brush each, no rules. Chunky figurines are the ideal first painting surface: no tiny details to frustrate, and mistakes look like decoration.
  • Assemble (everyone): chocolate egg into the bunny holder, animals arranged on the windowsill, photo taken before the cat intervenes.

Why this beats coloring books

The object is real. A printed bunny that the kid painted themselves stays on the shelf for years — and next spring it comes out again with a story attached. That feedback loop is the whole hook of making things.

Escalation for the hooked

Once the paint dries, the articulated animals are the reveal: a flexi bunny that actually moves feels like a toy, not a craft. The Flexi Toys & Learning collection has the safe, rounded candidates. For the pastel aesthetic, the Kawaii collection paints up beautifully in two colors.

Material note: plain PLA only for kids' projects — it is the stiff-but-safe standard, needs no ventilation drama, and survives being dropped on the floor more times than anyone will admit.
